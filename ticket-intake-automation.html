Overview

  • Email-based support intake: Focuses specifically on support requests submitted through email.
  • End-to-end email intake automation: Automates support intake from incoming emails through issue extraction, ticket creation, and assignment.
  • AI-driven triage: Uses AI to identify issue details and determine the appropriate handling path.
  • Clarification path: Requests missing details before creating a ticket.

Current Manual Intake Process

Shows the current manual flow and highlights the manual triage bottleneck before assignment and Jira ticket creation.

Key Challenges

  • Unstructured requests: Emails require manual review to understand the issue and capture key details.
  • Missing ticket trail: Some issues are handled through email without a logged ticket, limiting traceability.
  • Delayed acknowledgment: Users may not receive timely acknowledgment until work begins on the request.
  • Manual intake effort: Email review, ticket creation, and assignment are repetitive manual tasks that slow intake.

Use Case Diagram

Shows the end-to-end operational flow from incoming email to AI analysis, ticket creation, user acknowledgment, and assignment.

Business Value

  • Improves traceability: Ensures issues are logged, tracked, and auditable.
  • Accelerates response: Speeds acknowledgment and intake handling through automated processing.
  • Reduces manual effort: Lowers repetitive work for support and technical teams.

Technology Used

  • OpenAI API: Issue understanding and structured classification
  • Make: Workflow orchestration and automation
  • Outlook: Intake mailbox and notification emails
  • Jira Cloud: Application-related ticket creation
  • ServiceNow: Support ticket creation
  • Google Sheets: Assignment mapping and audit logging
