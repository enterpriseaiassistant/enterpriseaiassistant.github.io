Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Enterprise Assistant GPT prototype and related demo experience. The Enterprise Assistant is designed to answer application-specific questions, provide guided troubleshooting, and escalate unresolved issues to Jira or ServiceNow when requested by the user.

Purpose of This Prototype

The Enterprise Assistant is a demonstration prototype created to show how generative AI can support enterprise application support workflows. It is not a production support system.

Information Users May Provide

Users may choose to enter information such as:

  • Application names or system names
  • Descriptions of technical issues
  • Access request details
  • Troubleshooting responses
  • Information needed to create a Jira or ServiceNow ticket

How Information Is Used

Information entered into the Enterprise Assistant may be used to:

  • Provide application-specific answers using approved documentation
  • Guide users through troubleshooting steps
  • Prepare a draft support ticket for user review
  • Create a Jira or ServiceNow ticket only after user confirmation
  • Demonstrate prototype functionality

External Systems and Actions

The Enterprise Assistant may use connected actions to send information to external systems, including Jira and ServiceNow, when a user asks to escalate an issue or create a support ticket. These actions are intended only for demonstration and prototype purposes.

User Confirmation

The Enterprise Assistant is designed to ask for user confirmation before creating a Jira or ServiceNow ticket. Users should review ticket details before confirming ticket creation.

Information Users Should Not Enter

Users should not enter sensitive, confidential, regulated, or personal information into this prototype, including passwords, Social Security numbers, financial information, protected health information, production credentials, or confidential business data.

Data Retention

This prototype does not intentionally store user information on this website. However, information submitted through the GPT or sent to connected external systems may be processed or retained by those platforms according to their own policies and settings.

No Production Support Guarantee

This prototype is provided for demonstration purposes only. Tickets created through the demo may not represent real production support requests and should not be relied upon for official support.

Contact

For questions about this prototype or this Privacy Policy, please contact the creator through the project website or LinkedIn profile linked on the home page.
